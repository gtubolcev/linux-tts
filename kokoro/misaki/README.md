# misaki's English dictionaries

Generated by `../misaki-lexicon.py` from `us_gold.json`, `us_silver.json`,
`gb_gold.json` and `gb_silver.json` of [hexgrad/misaki](https://github.com/hexgrad/misaki)
at commit fba1236595f2d2bf21d414ba6e57d25256afada3 (Apache-2.0):

    python3 -I misaki-lexicon.py <dir with the four .json> misaki/

One word per line, sorted by its bytes: `word<TAB>phonemes`, or for a word read
by its part of speech `word<TAB>default<TAB>TAG=phonemes…` (an empty reading is
misaki's null).  `en-g2p.c` reads them.

misaki does not say where the pronunciations come from (its issues 107 and 113
ask); the files carry no other terms than the repository's licence.
